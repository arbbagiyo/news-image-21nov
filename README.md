# The World — Saturday 21 November 2026

Built for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121).

A newspaper front page drawn programmatically with Python + PIL (`render.py`),
1024×1024 PNG. Masthead "THE WORLD", red dateline "SATURDAY, 21 NOVEMBER 2026":

- **Main story:** Gen Z protests sweep Europe — young protesters rally in
  25 countries over wars, living costs and climate.
- **Second story:** Trump urges Nvidia to bring back Huang — the president
  calls on the chip giant to reinstate its former chief and "make a big
  apology".
- **Also:** Poland missile aftermath, the AI sell-off dragging on, COP31
  stalling in overtime, a 6.8 quake in Peru — plus the Iran war, Mamdani's
  Rikers plan and more.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; two runs are byte-identical.
